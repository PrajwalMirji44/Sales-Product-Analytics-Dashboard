# 📊 Sales & Product Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, product profitability, customer transactions, promotional activity, and sales trends across multiple dimensions from 2020 to 2024.

---

## 1. 📌 Project Overview

The **Sales & Product Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence solution built in Power BI to transform retail sales data into actionable insights.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics including **sales, profit, quantity sold, orders, product performance, promotional discounts, customer transactions, and geographic sales distribution**.

It is designed to help business stakeholders quickly identify high-performing products, evaluate profitability, compare promotional strategies, and understand changes in sales performance over time.

---

## 2. 🎯 Business Problem

Retail sales data can contain thousands of transactions across different products, customers, dates, and promotional campaigns, making it difficult to identify meaningful patterns from raw records.

The objective of this project was to build an interactive analytical dashboard that could answer questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
- Which products have the lowest sales and profitability?
- How does sales performance change over time?
- Which cities contribute most to overall sales?
- Which promotional categories have higher discount levels?
- How do profit and net sales relate to each other?
- Which products have strong sales volume versus strong profitability?

---

## 3. 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Used to build interactive dashboards, visualizations, KPIs, and analytical views.
- 🔄 **Power Query** – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and preparation before analysis.
- 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Used to create calculated measures, KPIs, aggregations, and dynamic analytical logic.
- 🗂️ **Data Modeling** – Used to structure the dataset and enable filtering and cross-analysis across customers, products, promotions, and dates.
- 📈 **Data Visualization** – Used bar charts, line charts, maps, scatter plots, tables, and KPI cards to communicate business insights.

## 5. ⭐ Features & Highlights

### 📌 Business KPIs

The dashboard provides a high-level overview of business performance through key metrics:

- **3.51K+ Orders**
- **₹100M+ Total Sales**
- **₹10M+ Total Profit**
- **5.8K+ Units Sold**

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of overall sales and profitability.

---

### 🛍️ Product Performance Analysis

The dashboard compares products based on:

- Total Sales
- Quantity Sold
- Profit

The **Apple iPhone 14** was the strongest-performing product in the dashboard, generating approximately:

- **₹21M in sales**
- **281 units sold**
- **₹2.1M in profit**

Other high-performing products included the **Apple MacBook Air, Sony Bravia 55" TV, Samsung Galaxy S21, and HP Pavilion Laptop**.

Bottom-performing products were also analyzed to identify products contributing relatively lower sales and profit.

---

### 📦 Top & Bottom Product Analysis

Dedicated Top 5 and Bottom 5 visualizations allow users to quickly compare products across different performance metrics.

The dashboard provides:

- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Bottom 5 Products by Sales
- Top 5 Products by Quantity
- Bottom 5 Products by Quantity
- Top 5 Products by Profit
- Bottom 5 Products by Profit

This makes it easier to distinguish between products that generate high revenue and products that generate high sales volume.

---

### 📈 Sales Trend Analysis

A time-series visualization tracks sales performance from **2020 through 2024**.

This allows users to identify:

- High-performing periods
- Low-performing periods
- Sales fluctuations
- Changes in transaction activity over time
- Longer-term sales patterns

The dashboard also includes date-range controls for more focused analysis.

---

### 🌍 Geographic Sales Analysis

A city-level map visualizes sales distribution across different locations.

This enables users to compare geographic performance and identify areas contributing significantly to overall sales.

---

### 💰 Profitability Analysis

The **Profit vs Net Sales** scatter plot provides a visual comparison between profitability and sales.

This helps identify the relationship between:

**Profit → Net Sales**

and allows users to identify products or transactions generating stronger financial returns.

---

### 🏷️ Promotion & Discount Analysis

The dashboard compares promotional categories based on discount activity.

Promotion categories included:

- Weekend Flash Sale
- Clearance Sale
- Summer Sale
- New Year Special
- Festive Diwali

This provides visibility into how different promotional campaigns are associated with discount levels.

---

### 🔎 Interactive Filtering

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

- 📅 Date
- 👤 Customer
- 📦 Product
- 🏷️ Promotion

Users can combine these filters to move from an overall business view to a specific customer, product, promotion, or time period.

---

## 6. 💡 Key Insights

Some of the key observations from the analysis include:

- **₹100M+ total sales** were recorded across the analyzed dataset.
- **₹10M+ total profit** was generated, providing an overall view of business profitability.
- The **Apple iPhone 14** was the strongest product by sales and profit, generating approximately **₹21M in sales and ₹2.1M in profit**.
- Product performance varies significantly when comparing **sales, quantity, and profit**, showing why a single KPI is not sufficient for product evaluation.
- The dashboard highlights both high-performing and low-performing products, supporting more focused product-level decision-making.
- Promotional categories show different discount levels, providing a basis for evaluating promotional activity.
- City-level analysis provides a geographic perspective on sales distribution.
- Sales trends across 2020–2024 reveal fluctuations in performance that can be explored using the dashboard's interactive date filters.
